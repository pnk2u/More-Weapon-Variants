## 1.1.0:
- **_NEW_** features:
    - Mobs now can use (_Cross_-)_bow_ variants
    - **Piglins** occasionally spawn with a _**Crimson** Crossbow_ equipped
    - **Skeletons**(/**Bogged**) now occasionally spawn with a _Bow_ variant made out of the wood of any in the biome available trees (e.g. **Skeletons** spawning in _Savanna_ biomes have a high chance of spawning with an _**Acacia** Bow_)
    - Mobs using (_Cross_-)_bow_ variants use the fitting _Arrow_ variant (and can also drop both the ranged weapon and the arrow as loot)
    - `1.21+`: **Bogged** drop _Tipped Poison Arrow_ variants
    - **Wither Skeletons** now have a high chance of spawning with either a _**Crimson** Stone Sword_ or a _**Crimson** Blackstone Sword_, which they can also drop.
    - **Piglins** spawning with a sword now have a chance of spawning with a _**Crimson** Golden Sword_
- `1.21.4`: Update to <ins>1.21.4</ins>
- `1.20.1`, `1.20.4`: Fix smelt-to-nugget recipes
